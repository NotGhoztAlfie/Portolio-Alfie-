**Week 4**
**Security, Ethics and Responsibility**

The Change Healthcare Cyber Attack:

A prime example of why cybersecurity is important. Hackers saw a flaw in the healthcare systems and exploited it because a remote access portal didn't have MFA (Multi Factor Authentication) turned on. This meant the hackers could simply log into accounts and change their passwords whilst implementing their own MFa to ensure there can't be any reversals. 

Millions and millions of people had their data stolen, couldn't get prescriptions and some couldn't get their treatment paid for due to the collapse of the system. Healthcare businesses like pharmacies and clinics went down for around 2 weeks, and Change Healthcare had to pay billions in reparations. This event showed how we need to be aware of all flaws in IT systems and to maintain everything to disable hackers from abusing parts of the system. 

The responsibilities you take on as an IT technician maintaining and building IT systems are:
1. Keeping your systems updated and patching anything that needs patching. 
2. Backing up the data you receive securely and ensuring there are access rights across the system to ensure potential data breaches are controlled.
